FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about LECFLOW.

What is LECFLOW?
LECFLOW is a free TA/Instructor lecture organiser and notes taking app for IIT Madras BS students. It syncs your public course calendars and brings live session links, recordings, and personal notes into one dashboard.
Do I need to make my personal calendar public?
No. LECFLOW only reads the public course calendar link published by IITM for each subject. Your personal Google Calendar is never accessed. You maintain full control over your privacy.
Does LECFLOW store the lecture videos?
No. Videos stay on the institute's Google Drive or YouTube. LECFLOW stores the links per lecture so you can find them instantly, even in later terms.
Can I take notes while attending a live session?
Yes. The notes editor sits next to the video with full Markdown formatting, KaTeX math rendering, code highlighting, autosave every 1.2 seconds, and one-click PDF export.
Is LECFLOW affiliated with IIT Madras?
No. LECFLOW is an independent student-built tool and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by IIT Madras.
How often does LECFLOW sync my calendars?
You can sync manually anytime with one click on the "Sync All" button. The app fetches the latest events from your course calendars on demand.
Can I use LECFLOW on my phone?
Yes. LECFLOW is fully responsive with a mobile-first design. The interface adapts to your screen size, making it easy to take notes, watch recordings, and track progress on any device.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your notes are private to your account. LECFLOW never shares your data with third parties. All data is stored securely and encrypted. You own your notes — export them anytime as PDF.
What happens when a term ends?
LECFLOW includes a term management system that archives recording links per subject per term. When a term ends, you can archive it and start fresh for the next term without losing access to past recordings.
Is LECFLOW really free?
Yes, completely free, no hidden costs, no credit card required. LECFLOW is built to help IITM BS students succeed.
